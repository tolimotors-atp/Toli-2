# Daylight Hero workshop assets

The built-in image generator produced two daylight variants from the existing workshop backgrounds. The site uses the wide variant at 1600 and 960 pixels and the portrait variant at 640 pixels. All three outputs are WebP. The separate vehicle artwork and dark workshop assets are unchanged.

## Wide variant prompt

Edit the reference into a photoreal daylight material and lighting variant of this exact automotive workshop, wide landscape 16:9. Preserve centered straight-on camera, vanishing point, room dimensions, ceiling truss positions, service bay positions, tool cabinets, lifts and polished floor geometry. Refinish ceiling panels and columns in pearl white and metallic silver, with visibly defined light graphite beams and trusses. Bright neutral white linear LED fixtures, softened daylight from rear glazed service doors and high windows, gentle silver grey polished concrete with realistic diffuse reflections. Retain only a few small controlled TOLI red column bases, distant tool drawers and horizontal stripes; quiet warm-bronze floor markers. Medium contrast with readable but soft architecture, especially across the central area where the site will overlay dark heading text. Match original balance of ceiling, rear wall, and floor, preserve clear unobstructed center aisle and empty foreground. Edge-to-edge image, clean contemporary automotive service center. Environment only, absolutely no cars, people, letters, signage, logos, panels of UI or watermarks. Strong material realism and recognizable equipment, no haze, wireframes or overexposure.

## Portrait variant prompt

Edit this exact portrait workshop photo into the same room in bright daylight for a mobile website background. Keep the centered vanishing point, perspective and truss and column geometry. Frame in portrait 4:5 with visible ceiling and rear bays in the upper two thirds and silver concrete floor in lower third; trim excess foreground only. Refinish ceiling panels and columns in pearl white and metallic silver, with visibly defined soft graphite trusses. Bright white linear LED fixtures, daylight from rear glazed service doors, visible side lifts and neatly arranged cabinets. Keep a few small restrained TOLI red column-base and tool drawer accents, faint warm bronze floor guides and gentle diffuse floor reflections. Medium contrast, calm central surfaces behind dark heading copy. Clean realistic automotive workshop, empty foreground for existing separate vehicle cutouts. Environment only, no cars, people, lettering, logos, UI, or watermark. Full bleed. No wireframes or washed-out whites.
