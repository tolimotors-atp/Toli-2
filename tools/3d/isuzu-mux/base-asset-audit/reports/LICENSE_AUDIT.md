## Warehouse: strongest unapproved lead

[General Model License](https://3dwarehouse.sketchup.com/tou#license) viewed directly in the browser, dated March 18, 2015. Its section 1 allows modifications and combined business works; section 2 restricts standalone distribution, requires retained notices and protection from unauthorized copying. This does not establish the uploader’s underlying authorship. Whether this site’s ordinary retrievable converted model satisfies those conditions remains unresolved. No categorical claim that every GLB or all commercial Warehouse use is forbidden is made.

The Terms of Use page viewed at https://3dwarehouse.sketchup.com/tou identifies version 2.0, updated September 30, 2026. No terms were accepted, account created or download control activated.

Before L3: document the actual geometry creator and authorization chain; establish terms covering modification/conversion and the intended commercial public model delivery; retain required notices and determine attribution. No messages were sent. This is the exact unresolved gate, not a request to buy an asset.

## Sketchfab CC label and provenance

[CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/) permits commercial adaptation/distribution with appropriate credit, a license link and change notices. These freedoms depend on an authorized licensor and do not supply all other rights. C02 explicitly credits another base creator; original-public-page permission for derivative relicensing was not found. Its newer body is independently a poor match. C03 offers no verified public reuse license/download. C04 is a pickup and has an unresolved ownership claim: it is rejected without adjudicating that claim.
