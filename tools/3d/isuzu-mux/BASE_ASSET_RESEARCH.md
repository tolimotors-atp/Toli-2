## Critical license result

[3DModels portal terms](https://3dmodels.org/license-terms/), sections 3.5–3.9, allow project/software uses subject to preventing separation/extraction of the model; they do not establish permission for an ordinary publicly retrievable GLB. [Additional terms](https://3dmodels.org/terms-and-conditions/) permit business modifications but restrict external distribution and unprotected extraction. A displayed Extended License option does **not** by itself prove that those restrictions are removed. Intended commercial web use therefore remains **UNCLEAR / NOT CLEARED** for candidates 1–3; obtain written, use-specific permission and its price.

[Free3D terms](https://free3d.com/royalty-free-license), II.4 and II.7, combine editorial restrictions with protected-software distribution requirements and an explicit open-format/WebGL restriction. Candidate 4 has an Editorial Only notice. It is not suitable for the proposed ordinary commercial GLB workflow under the reviewed listing/terms.

[Warehouse general terms](https://3dwarehouse.sketchup.com/tos) are separate from the SketchUp application's license. Public indexed terms discuss business uses, but the exact asset's authorship chain and permission for this distribution were not established. Do not infer that a free download or a platform license proves the uploader owns the underlying geometry. No evidence of infringement is alleged; provenance is simply unverified.
